Our story

Started with one bad
buying experience.

In {{ brand.founded }} one of our founders bought a used SUV that looked immaculate. Three months later the transmission went, and the "complete papers" turned out to be missing an encumbrance release. The dealer stopped answering calls.

That's a common enough story in the Philippines that most buyers just accept the risk. We didn't want to. {{ brand.name }} started as a small lot with four units and one non-negotiable rule: if we wouldn't put a family member in it, we don't sell it.

Today we carry {{ stats()?.available ?? 'dozens of' }} inspected units across {{ stats()?.makes ?? 'several' }} brands, and that rule hasn't changed. Roughly a third of the cars we appraise get rejected outright. It makes our stock smaller. It also means you can buy from us without holding your breath.
